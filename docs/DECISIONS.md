## #742 - The debt-payoff path names a balance the bank stopped sharing (L.19 surface 5) (2026-09-16)

**Context.** TASKS L.19 listed five surfaces DECISIONS #305 left printing a figure over a frozen (`feedDroppedAt`) balance with nothing said; slice A (#304) closed (1) and (4), #305 closed the Today feed, the undatable loan and the PDF export, and named the debt-payoff path as the one surface it deliberately left open. `loadDebtAccounts` mapped each liability to the engine's `DebtInput` and dropped `feedDroppedAt` on the way — the same server-side NARROWING #305 diagnosed three times. **Decision — carry the fact on the READ PATH'S type, never into the engine.** New `DebtAccount extends DebtInput` (`kind: 'card' | 'loan'`, `frozenSince: string | null`) is what `loadDebtAccounts` returns and what the planner and both Ask answers accept; `planDebtPayoff` and `solveDebtFreeByDate` still take `DebtInput` and never read the stamp — locked by a `toEqual` of the plan with and without it. Stance unchanged from #305: DISCLOSE, ADJUST NOTHING — the save action survives, the figures are byte-identical, the note sits ABOVE the save control so the reader sees what the persisted total rests on before persisting it.

**Its own builder, `frozenDebtPlanNote`, not `frozenTotalNote`.** A payoff plan is not a total: the frozen balance is the STARTING POINT every month of the amortisation walks forward from, so the claim is about what the plan is worked out from, not what a sum contains. `figureLabel` is required (the #305 rule): "this payoff plan" on the planner and the forward answer, "the total debt and the extra needed to clear it" on the by-date answer's reachable branches — and "the total debt" alone on its unreachable branch, which prints no extra (critic P2-1). Rows resolve against the set the surface actually prints (`perDebt` on the planner; `Σ max(0, balance)` on the by-date answer, so a zero-balance frozen row is outside the figure and unnamed). No `ownership` argument, deliberately: the personal snapshot is never household-merged, so every row is the reader's own by construction; the header names the day that changes as the day this builder must gain one.

**Kind decides the mechanism — and a LOAN CLAIMS NO DIRECTION.** A card that is still in use has taken charges AND payments since the drop, so its real balance "may be higher or lower". The first cut said a loan's balance "can only be lower" (payments come off, nothing goes on). The hostile critic (P1-1) showed that false on rows this codebase itself produces: `plaid-map.ts` folds every non-mortgage Plaid `loan` subtype into `LOAN` — including the revolving `line of credit` and `home equity`, whose real balance RISES on a draw — and even an amortising loan accrues interest daily and can carry a missed payment above the frozen figure. The understating claim is the dangerous direction (the reader told their debt can only be smaller than shown). The loan branch now matches `frozenLoanNote`'s precedent: "nothing about this loan has been confirmed since, so the real balance may not be the one used here." A mixed card+loan note says the Accounts remedy ONCE, over the set (critic P2-2).

**Alternatives rejected.** (a) Excluding frozen debts from the plan — would print a debt-free date the reader is not on track for and stop the app telling someone to pay a card they still owe (#305's argument, one obligation type across). (b) Widening `DebtInput` itself — puts a data-integrity fact into a pure engine's input where nothing reads it; the extension type keeps the engine's contract untouched and lets the compiler ask each consumer for the fact. (c) A cross-surface "N of your accounts are frozen" banner instead of a per-figure note — the reader saving a goal needs the claim beside the number the goal will persist, not elsewhere on the page.
