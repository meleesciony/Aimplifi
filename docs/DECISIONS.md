**Kind decides the mechanism — and a LOAN CLAIMS NO DIRECTION.** A card that is still in use has taken charges AND payments since the drop, so its real balance "may be higher or lower". The understating claim is the dangerous direction (the reader told their debt can only be smaller than shown). The loan branch now matches `frozenLoanNote`'s precedent: "nothing about this loan has been confirmed since, so the real balance may not be the one used here." A mixed card+loan note says the Accounts remedy ONCE, over the set (critic P2-2).
