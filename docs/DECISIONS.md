considered. Append-only.

| 29 | R5 | Minimum-path interest upgraded from the labeled simple-monthly approximation (carried × APR/12) to the **average-daily-balance method** (ROADMAP #3, supersedes #5/#21). Per card not paid in full: interest = round(DPR × Σ daily balances) over the next cycle [close → close+1mo], DPR = APR/10000/365, balance = full statement until the minimum posts on the due date then the carried balance after; grace-gated (paid in full → $0); new purchases not projected. Pure primitive `averageDailyBalanceInterestCents` in money.ts (own known-answer tests); the engine derives cycleDays/daysUntilDue from the statement's close+due dates (estimate path uses nextCycleClose/nextDue). Every pinned value recomputed by hand and updated with its test (EDGE_CASES §I; seed $65.76→$67.36, §I $59.30→$61.08, critic5 §I $58.81, S8 $12.23, N6 $19.17/$18.74) | The v1 formula was always labeled a documented approximation awaiting ADB (#5/#21, STATUS #2). ADB is the method real issuers use and the constitution's "financial math is sacred" priority; it is hand-verifiable and e2e-verifiable. The value is cycle-date dependent (two §I anchors differ), which is correct — the doc and tests now pin both. Grace-gating preserves the autopay-in-full = $0 behavior the old `carried` formula gave for free; modeling interest on the full balance until the minimum posts captures the lost-grace reality the flat formula missed |
